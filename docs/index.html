Why

  • Zero dependencies. No node_modules tree, no Handlebars, no Inquirer. Just Node.
  • Runs real Yeoman generators, too. No extra dependency for that either – jen borrows yeoman-generator, mem-fs and EJS straight from the generator's own install.
  • No dialog. Params come from the command line. Nothing asks you questions – Yeoman generators included.
  • Templates with logic, for free. Template literals already do conditionals and loops.
  • Plan first, then act. Every run shows the plan before writing anything.
  • Deterministic. Same params, same output. Every time.
  • Language-agnostic. Scaffolds C++, CMake, Rust, shaders, or anything else that's text.
  • Try before you install. --from fetches a pack or generator via npm, runs it once, and cleans up after itself.

Not just your own generators

jen also runs real Yeoman generators, and can fetch one on the fly without ever touching your project's own dependencies.

$ jen --from generator-code code --extensionType=command-ts --extensionDisplayName="My Extension"
